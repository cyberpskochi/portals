Users & permissions

New Google accounts that try to sign in appear here as pending. Tick the analyzers they may use, Work Done access and whether they may replace an existing backup, set status to approved and press Save. Blocking a user signs them out immediately.

Activity log (sign-ins, reports, backups, admin changes)

The full log is also kept in the Google Sheet CPS Portal — Users & Logs in the admin's Drive. The IP is the public internet address of the user's connection.

Encrypted backups

Backups are made only when a report is generated. Deleted or replaced backups are moved to the _replaced folder in Drive (recoverable by the admin); empty that folder in Drive to remove them for good. Only the admin can clear backups — every deletion is logged.
